## How to Request Account and Data Deletion

We provide two straightforward methods for users to permanently delete their account and associated data:

### Method 1: In-App Deletion (Instant)
1. Open the **FindAura** game on your device.
2. Tap the **Settings (HUD)** icon on the home screen or game screen.
3. Scroll down and tap **"Delete Account & Reset Data"**.
4. Confirm your selection. Your cloud profile, cloud save progress, badges, and local data will be deleted immediately.

---

### Method 2: Email Request (Without App Installed)
If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you can request manual deletion of your account and cloud data:
1. Send an email to **rawviai@gmail.com** with the subject line: `Account Deletion Request - FindAura`.
2. Include the email address used for Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In (or your in-game User ID if known).
3. We will process your deletion request and erase all associated records from Supabase and our database within **7 business days**.

---

## Data That Will Be Deleted
* User profile records and unique authentication IDs
* Cloud-saved game progress (levels completed, hints, unlocked worlds, badges)
* Any email address linked during OAuth sign-in

## Data That May Be Retained
* Non-identifiable crash analytics logs aggregated by Google Firebase (retained temporarily for general bug-fixing according to Google's standard retention policies).
