Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the Application, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: If you choose to sign in (via Google Sign-In, Apple Sign-In, or Supabase), we may receive your basic profile information such as your name, email address, and unique user ID.
  • Support Inquiries: If you contact us for support, we may collect your email address and any information you provide in your message.

B. Gameplay & Local Data

Level progress, unlocked worlds, badges, hints, and game settings are saved locally on your device (and synced securely with your account if signed in).

C. Automatically Collected Information & Third-Party SDKs

When you use the Application, certain information is collected automatically by third-party services integrated into the app:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): May collect device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / IDFA), coarse location, IP address, and ad interaction data to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements.
  • Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics: Collects crash logs, device model, operating system version, and anonymous usage metrics to help us diagnose errors and improve app performance.
  • Supabase: Manages secure cloud authentication and user progress synchronization.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the gameplay experience.
  • Save and sync your game progress across devices.
  • Display relevant in-game advertisements to keep the app free.
  • Monitor app stability, fix crashes, and troubleshoot bugs.
  • Respond to customer service and support inquiries.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following scenarios:

5. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Local Data: You can delete locally stored game progress at any time by clearing the app data in your device settings or uninstalling the app.
  • Account Deletion: If you created an account and wish to delete your account and associated data permanently, you can do so directly within the app settings or by contacting us at support@rawvi.com.
