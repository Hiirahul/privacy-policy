Privacy Policy for Mathizzle

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mathizzle ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile application. We built Mathizzle to be playable without creating an account, and we have kept data collection to what is needed to run the app, show ads, and understand basic usage.

1. Information We Collect

Mathizzle does not require you to sign up or create an account. You can play immediately as a guest, and your game progress (levels, coins, XP, streaks) is stored locally on your device only. We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal account information.

However, the App uses third-party services (described below) that may automatically collect certain technical information, such as:

  • Device identifiers (such as your Advertising ID)
  • Device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language/region)
  • Usage data (screens viewed, features used, session length, crash and performance data)

We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise GPS location.

2. Local, Guest-Only Gameplay Data

Your in-app progress -- including level completion, coins, XP, and streaks -- is stored only on your device. We do not upload this progress to any server, and there is currently no cloud save, login, or cross-device sync. If you uninstall the App or clear its data, this progress is permanently lost and cannot be recovered.

3. Advertising

Child-Safe Advertising: Mathizzle is enrolled in Google Play's Families program. All advertisements are served with child-directed treatment enabled and are non-personalized (NPA). We do not use interest-based targeting, behavioral advertising, or remarketing. All ads are rated suitable for general audiences (G-rated content only).

Mathizzle displays ads through Google AdMob to keep the App free to play. In accordance with the Google Play Families Policy and applicable children's privacy laws (COPPA, GDPR), our AdMob configuration is set to:

  • Child-directed treatment: enabled
  • Ad content rating: G (General Audiences)
  • Personalized advertising: disabled (non-personalized ads only)
  • Interest-based advertising and remarketing: disabled

For more information on how Google's advertising products handle data, see Google's Advertising Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Analytics

We use analytics services (such as Google Firebase Analytics) to understand how players use Mathizzle -- for example, which levels are played most, how long sessions last, and where crashes occur. This helps us fix bugs and improve the game. Analytics data is aggregated and is not used to personally identify you.

See Firebase's Privacy and Security policy for more detail on how this data is handled.

5. Children's Privacy

Mathizzle is a family-friendly math puzzle game available to users of all ages, including children. We take children's privacy seriously and have implemented the following protections:

  • All ads are served with child-directed treatment and are non-personalized (no behavioral profiling).
  • All ad content is rated G (General Audiences) -- no mature or inappropriate content is served.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal data from children beyond the anonymous technical data described in Section 1.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers to build profiles on child users.
  • Our advertising implementation complies with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation for children (GDPR-K).

If you believe a child has provided personal information beyond what is described above, please contact us using the details in Section 10 so we can address it promptly.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Limited technical data is shared only with the third-party advertising and analytics providers named above (Google AdMob and Firebase), solely for the purposes of displaying non-personalized, child-safe ads and understanding app usage, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

7. Data Retention

Since gameplay progress is stored only on your device, it exists for as long as the App remains installed with its data intact. Data collected by our third-party advertising and analytics partners (Google AdMob, Firebase) is retained according to their own respective policies.

8. Your Choices

  • Since all ads in Mathizzle are already non-personalized, no opt-out action is required. However, you can also reset your Advertising ID at any time in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads).
  • You can clear the App's local data or uninstall the App at any time, which removes all locally stored progress.
  • You can uninstall the App to stop all data collection associated with it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example as we add new features such as cloud save or accounts. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
